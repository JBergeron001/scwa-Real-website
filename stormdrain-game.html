    What washes into our creeks?

    Storm drains in St. Charles County are not connected to a treatment plant. Whatever goes in flows to the nearest creek, then on to the Missouri and Mississippi Rivers.

      Catching raindrops is good too: a real rain garden soaks up rainwater so less of it rushes into the drain.
